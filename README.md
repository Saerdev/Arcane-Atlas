# Arcane Atlas

**Website:** [Arcane Atlas](https://saerdev.github.io/Arcane-Atlas/)

Arcane Atlas is a Warframe arcane price aggregator. It puts Warframe.market buy and sell listings for all arcanes on one page so you can compare prices without opening a bunch of tabs.

You can search and sort arcanes, compare rank 0 and max-rank prices, check listing counts and reported 48-hour trading volume, and hover over an arcane to see its effects and where to get it. Each arcane also links to its Warframe.market listing and Wiki page.

Prices are pulled on a schedule, not in real time. Volume data comes from reported market statistics and may not always be available.

This is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Digital Extremes or Warframe.market. Market data comes from Warframe.market; arcane information comes from WFCD. See [third-party notices](site/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for credits.

How to read the board

Sell is the cheapest current listing from a seller; Buy is the highest offer from a buyer. The spread is the sell price minus the buy price, when both exist. Values are per unit when listings use bulk trades. Listings counts available buy/sell offers; 48h volume sums Warframe.market’s reported closed-order activity over the preceding 48 hours. In Compare Both view, volume includes all ranks; single-rank views show only the selected rank. Closed orders do not guarantee a completed player-to-player trade.

⌁
About freshness

The public site reads a snapshot updated by a scheduled process approximately every 30 minutes, subject to API availability and hosting delays. “Online” means online or in-game at the time of that scan, not necessarily right now. 48h volume is collected separately and normally refreshed no more than once daily to reduce API traffic. If the legacy statistics feed is unavailable, the site displays a dash instead of a fabricated volume. The update button checks for a newer published snapshot; it does not trigger a market scan. Arcane effects and acquisition sources come from WFCD item data and may omit vendors or time-limited events. Use each arcane’s Wiki link for full acquisition methods.

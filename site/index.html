How to read the board

48h Avg is a volume-weighted average of hourly reported closed-order prices over the preceding 48 hours. Last is the median reported price in the most recent hour with closed-order activity, which may be older than one hour if no recent trades were reported. 48h Volume counts reported closed orders. These are not live listing prices or verified player-to-player trades. Rank 0 and max-rank values are separate; Compare Both volume includes all ranks.

⌁
About freshness

Warframe.market updates its 48-hour closed-order statistics hourly when trading volume is nonzero. Arcane Atlas republishes a snapshot on an approximately hourly schedule, subject to scheduler, API, and hosting delays. An individual arcane’s last traded hour can be older if no trades were reported. The update button only checks for a newer published snapshot; it does not trigger a market scan. Arcane effects and acquisition sources come from WFCD and may omit vendors or time-limited events.
